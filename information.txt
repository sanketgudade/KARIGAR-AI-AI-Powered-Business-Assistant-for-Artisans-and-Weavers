1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & VISION
====================================================================================================

KARIGAR AI (कारीगर AI) is a purpose-built, multimodal business operating system and digital partner 
designed for traditional Indian handloom weavers, potters, metalcraft artisans, and rural craft clusters.

Unlike standard e-commerce aggregators that treat artisans as passive suppliers and take 30% to 50% 
platform margins, KARIGAR AI equips artisans with their own autonomous business management team:
- Multilingual voice-driven digital cataloguing.
- Deep cultural heritage preservation.
- Scientific cost accounting and market price intelligence.
- Reverse-image visual discovery for buyers.
- Empathetic bulk negotiation intelligence that defends artisan margins.
- Milestone-backed escrow protection.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT 7.1 BREAKDOWN
====================================================================================================

Problem Statement 7.1 identifies the acute structural vulnerabilities faced by traditional Indian craftspeople:

1. THE DIGITAL & LANGUAGE BARRIER:
   Artisans communicate in regional languages and spoken dialects (Marathi, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu). 
   Existing commerce platforms require structured English data entry, studio photography, and written 
   marketing descriptions, which rural craftspeople have never been taught.

2. PREDATORY MIDDLEMEN & DESTRUCTIVE MARGINS:
   Without real-time access to retail pricing and wholesale benchmarks, rural weavers sell heirloom 
   textiles to middlemen for ₹2,500 - ₹3,000, which are subsequently resold in urban showrooms for 
   ₹8,000 - ₹12,000. Commercial intermediaries capture 60% to 75% of the economic value.

3. UNDERVALUED LABOR & ACCIDENTAL LOSS-MAKING:
   Artisans rarely factor in skilled labor hours, yarn prep, or packaging overheads. When wholesale 
   buyers request large bulk orders, artisans frequently agree to catastrophic volume discounts 
   that cause them to work for weeks below minimum wage.

4. LOSS OF INTANGIBLE CULTURAL HERITAGE:
   Every authentic handloom product embodies motifs (e.g., Asawali, Mor, Bangadi), centuries-old 
   dyeing methods, and pit-loom techniques. Traditional retail strips away this provenance, turning 
   irreplaceable cultural heritage into commoditized goods.

3. SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE & UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITIONS (USPs)
====================================================================================================

KARIGAR AI transforms the artisan from an exploited laborer into an empowered digital business owner:

USP 1: SOVEREIGN 3-GATE APPROVAL SYSTEM
Every AI capability is bounded by an immutable "Approval Gate". AI never auto-publishes listings, 
never auto-sets selling rates, and never auto-sends counteroffers. The artisan holds 100% authority.

USP 2: MULTIMODAL CULTURAL GROUNDING (VOICE-FIRST)
Artisans simply speak in their mother tongue (e.g., Marathi). Speech-to-Text (Whisper) + Indic 
Translation (Bhashini) transcribe and translate the voice note. Computer vision observes visible 
motifs while strictly respecting artisan claims regarding silk purity and GI certifications.

USP 3: SCIENTIFIC PRODUCTION COST ACCOUNTING & LIVING WAGE DEFENSE
Agent 2 breaks down production into Raw Materials + (Labor Hours × Fair Hourly Wage) + Overheads. 
It establishes a true cost floor so artisans never sell below their investment.

USP 4: CONFIDENTIAL FLOOR PRICE SYSTEM (ZERO DATA LEAKAGE)
The system calculates a public listing price and a secret "Confidential Minimum Floor". The floor 
is protected at the database and serialization levels. Buyers, API clients, and network traffic 
NEVER receive or see the confidential floor.

USP 5: EMPATHETIC VALUE-ARTICULATING COUNTEROFFER GENERATOR
When a buyer offers a below-floor price (e.g., offering ₹3,000 for a saree with a ₹4,200 floor), 
Agent 4 does not simply say "No". It drafts a polite, culturally rooted response in the artisan's voice 
explaining the 20 days of hand-weaving and offers a sustainable wholesale rate (e.g., ₹4,500).

USP 6: VISUAL MATCHING VIA VECTOR RETRIEVAL
Buyers can upload reference photos of crafts they admire. The platform computes image embeddings 
and matches them against registered artisan products using vector similarity, eliminating the need 
for buyers to know technical handloom terminology.

USP 7: CONFIRMED-ESCROW FINANCIAL REPORTING
Unconfirmed RFQs and preliminary inquiries NEVER inflate the artisan's reported income. Revenue 
is only recognized upon funded escrow deposits, ensuring financial transparency.

4. THE 4 SPECIALIZED AI AGENTS
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AGENT 1: MULTIMODAL STORY & CATALOGUING AGENT (Gate 1)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Converts photos and native-tongue voice recordings into verified digital product listings.
- Inputs:
  * Product photographs (front view, zari border, pallu motif).
  * Voice note recorded in Marathi, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or English.
- AI Pipeline:
  * Speech-to-Text: Whisper audio transcription.
  * Translation: Indic translation model (Bhashini architecture).
  * Computer Vision: Palette extraction, motif detection (e.g., peacock, lotus), weave irregularity inspection.
- Grounding Rule: Vision model observations are clearly separated from artisan declarations (e.g., pure silk claims).
- Output: Draft product title, category, cultural story, technical specifications.
- State: Held strictly at 'DRAFT' until the artisan approves via Gate 1.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AGENT 2: FAIR PRICE & MARKET INTELLIGENCE AGENT (Gate 2)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Establishes fair commercial valuation using bottom-up cost accounting + regional benchmarks.
- Cost Model:
  * Material Cost: ₹1,500 (mulberry silk warp/weft + tested gold zari).
  * Labor Valuation: 20 days × ₹140/day = 20 hrs × ₹50/hr = ₹1,000.
  * Overheads & Packaging: ₹300.
  * Total Production Cost: ₹2,800.
- Market Intelligence Engine:
  * Scans and indexes 5 verified regional craft listings from portals like CraftBazaar and WeaverStory.
  * Computes Min (₹4,500), Median (₹5,200), and Max (₹6,000) benchmarks.
- Output:
  * Recommended Public Listing Price: ₹5,000 (44% gross margin).
  * Confidential Floor Price: ₹4,200 (secret loss-prevention boundary).
- State: Product transitions to 'APPROVED_LISTING' upon Gate 2 approval.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AGENT 3: VISUAL DISCOVERY & BUYER MATCHING AGENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Enables corporate, wholesale, and retail buyers to discover authentic artisan creations.
- Capabilities:
  * Multimodal visual search: Reverse-image matching using dense vector embeddings (CLIP / ResNet).
  * Faceted search: Filter by GI cluster (Yeola, Kanchipuram, Kot Jewar), craft category, lead time.
  * Automated Quotation Generator: Generates transparent bulk pricing tiers based on quantity requested.
- Security Guarantee: Public buyer API responses NEVER expose internal costs or confidential floor prices.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AGENT 4: NEGOTIATION & COMMERCIAL INTELLIGENCE AGENT (Gate 3)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Guards artisan financial viability during wholesale and retail price negotiations.
- Threat Detection Algorithm:
  * Compares buyer offer against Confidential Floor Price.
  * If Buyer Offer < Floor Price: Flags an immediate RED ALERT (Threat: BELOW_CONFIDENTIAL_FLOOR).
- Comparative Profit Simulation:
  * Simulates accepted buyer offer vs. recommended AI counteroffer.
  * Example: At ₹3,000 offer -> ₹200 profit/saree (₹10,000 for 50 pieces - unfair wage).
  * At ₹4,500 counteroffer -> ₹1,700 profit/saree (₹85,000 total net profit - preserves 38% margin).
- Counteroffer Response Generator:
  * Drafts an empathetic, polite response explaining production labor and proposing a volume discount.
- Transmission Rule: Response is held at 'PENDING_ARTISAN'. It is NEVER transmitted without Gate 3 approval.

8. END-TO-END 9-STEP BUSINESS LIFECYCLE
====================================================================================================

The entire application workflow is fully validated and executes without human intervention in 
automated integration testing (`backend/validate_lifecycle.py`):

[STEP 1] ARTISAN ONBOARDING & CLUSTER PROFILE
Artisan registers with name, phone, language (e.g. Marathi), and craft cluster (Yeola, Nashik).

[STEP 2] MULTIMODAL VOICE-FIRST UPLOAD (AGENT 1)
Artisan speaks in Marathi ("माझं नाव मीना जाधव आहे...") and uploads craft photos. Agent 1 transcribes, 
translates, and synthesizes a cultural story. Product remains held at status 'DRAFT'.

[STEP 3] SOVEREIGN CATALOGUE APPROVAL (GATE 1)
Artisan reviews and confirms the listing. Product transitions from 'DRAFT' to 'APPROVED_LISTING'.

[STEP 4] COST ACCOUNTING & BENCHMARK PRICING (AGENT 2 & GATE 2)
Agent 2 calculates material costs (₹1,500) + skilled labor (20 hrs × ₹50/hr = ₹1,000) + overheads (₹300) = ₹2,800.
Retrieves 5 regional comparables (Median: ₹5,200). Artisan approves listing price (₹5,000) and sets 
confidential floor (₹4,200). Product transitions to 'ACTIVE_MARKET'.

[STEP 5] VISUAL DISCOVERY & BUYER RFQ (AGENT 3)
Buyer searches by reverse image. Finds the Paithani Saree (72% visual match). Submits bulk RFQ 
for 50 sarees for a corporate Diwali gifting program.

[STEP 6] BUYER NEGOTIATION & THREAT FLAGGING (AGENT 4)
Buyer submits an aggressive below-floor offer of ₹3,000/saree. Agent 4 flags: BELOW_CONFIDENTIAL_FLOOR.
Simulates margin loss and drafts a polite counteroffer at ₹4,500/saree (protecting ₹85,000 net profit).
Response held at 'PENDING_ARTISAN'.

[STEP 7] SOVEREIGN COUNTEROFFER TRANSMISSION (GATE 3)
Artisan reviews threat rationale, checks profit simulation, and approves the ₹4,500 counteroffer. 
Only then is the message transmitted to the buyer.

[STEP 8] BUYER DEAL ACCEPTANCE & ESCROW DEPOSIT
Buyer accepts the artisan's counteroffer. Escrow payment of ₹2,25,000 (50 × ₹4,500) is locked. 
Order #ORD-2026-8841 is confirmed with status 'ESCROW_PAID'.

[STEP 9] 6-STAGE PRODUCTION LIFECYCLE & SETTLEMENT
Order progresses through:
1. Confirmed -> 2. Materials Ready -> 3. Pit-Loom Weaving -> 4. Quality Inspection -> 
5. Ready to Ship -> 6. Dispatched via India Post SpeedPost -> 7. Delivered.
Upon delivery, escrow funds are released to the artisan. Total confirmed revenue updates to ₹2,73,000.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
====================================================================================================

A foundational requirement of Problem Statement 7.1 is that an artisan's minimum acceptable price 
and internal production breakdown MUST NEVER BE DISCLOSED to potential buyers.

KARIGAR AI implements defense-in-depth protection:

1. OBJECT-LEVEL SERIALIZATION SHIELDS:
   In `backend/app/models/__init__.py`, `Product.to_dict()` accepts an optional flag: `include_confidential=False` (default).
   - If `include_confidential=False` (Buyer calls):
     * `cost` is completely omitted.
     * `confidential_min_price` is completely omitted.
   - If `include_confidential=True` (Artisan authenticated calls):
     * Detailed costs and confidential floors are included for dashboard display.

2. ROUTE-LEVEL ISOLATION:
   - `/api/buyer/*` endpoints strictly query public representations and never load cost models into response payloads.
   - `/api/artisan/*` endpoints enforce artisan ownership before returning financial floor settings.

3. NETWORK-LEVEL TEST VERIFICATION:
   `backend/test_api.py` (Test 8) explicitly queries the buyer endpoint and asserts:
   ```python
   assert 'confidential_floor' not in buyer_data
   assert 'product_costs' not in buyer_data
   ```
   Ensuring zero data leakage across network boundaries.
